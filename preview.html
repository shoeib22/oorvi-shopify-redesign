Ask us anything
What makes cold pressed oil different?+

Cold pressing extracts oil mechanically from seeds, without the refining, bleaching, and deodorising used for many refined oils. The seed’s natural aroma and character remain.

Cold pressed or wood pressed?+

Our cold pressed oils use a steel expeller. Our wood pressed range uses a traditional slow-turning ghani. Both are single pressed and naturally settled. Choose by the flavour you enjoy and the meals you cook.

Why does my oil look a little cloudy?+

Our oils are naturally settled. A little sediment or a change in colour can be part of their natural character. Coconut oil can also solidify in cooler temperatures.

How should I store my bottle?+

Keep it tightly closed in a cool, dry place, away from direct sunlight. Follow the storage instructions and best-before date on your bottle.
