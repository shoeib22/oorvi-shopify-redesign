# Oorvi — a little tradition, a lot of goodness

The redesign positions Oorvi as a modern Indian pantry brand. The product remains the hero; traditional pressing becomes a simple, understandable story rather than a luxury-product manifesto.

## Direction

- Forest green `#244a35`, warm cream `#faf7ed`, butter `#f2e9bc`, and sage `#e5eadb`.
- DM Sans for friendly, substantial headlines and body copy; Bodoni Moda italic for a few expressive words. Fonts are hosted locally with their Open Font Licenses.
- Original Oorvi primary and secondary logos, the original logomark, custom SVG icons, restrained rounded corners, generous spacing, and tactile natural-light photography.
- Gramiyaa inspired the emphasis on products and clear oil choices; Nune inspired seed and pressing education; Country Delight inspired everyday kitchen relevance. This is an original Oorvi composition.

## Homepage journey

1. Large generated bottle still life and a direct shopping invitation.
2. A concise ingredient and process strip.
3. Four featured oils, live Shopify prices where available, and cold/wood pressed filters.
4. An illustrated three-step pressing story.
5. A cooking-based oil finder: everyday, traditional, or light.
6. A generated Indian breakfast scene that connects oils to everyday meals.
7. Practical oil questions, followed by newsletter signup and a useful footer.

The collection, product, shopping bag, story, contact, search, error, and customer pages share this visual language. Product variants, quantities, galleries, sorting, contact forms, newsletter signup, and customer forms remain connected to Shopify.

## Readability and original branding update

The type scale was increased across headings, navigation, descriptions, product cards, controls, forms, and footer text. Main body copy is generally 16?17 px; supporting product text is generally 14?15 px. Mobile controls and card layouts were adjusted for the larger text.

The original logo SVGs were copied without redrawing from the local Oorvi archive at `Documents/oorvi-redesign/public/assets/logo/SVG Files`. The primary logo appears in the header/mobile menu, the secondary in the footer, and the original mark is the favicon. The revised campaign references the local original coconut mockup at `Documents/oorvi-redesign-source-assets/label-designs-raw/Label Designs/Oils/1Litre/Mockups/Wood Press coconut oil/Wood Press Coconut Front Png.png` and the original groundnut bottle asset.
